About me

I'm Margie, a third-year Information Technology student who enjoys making sense of ideas and turning them into something useful.

I was born on October 29, 2006, and I live in Barangay Palo-Alto, Calamba City, Laguna. I am currently studying Information Technology at City College of Calamba, where I am building a strong foundation in web development, networking and system administration.

One of my strengths is my ability to analyze and understand problems. Whether it's a network layout, a block of code or a design brief, I like to study how things work, break complex tasks into smaller steps, and work through them patiently until I find a solution.

Right now, I am focused on improving my coding skills through coursework, personal practice and hands-on projects. I have also created graphic design work in Photoshop, including coffee shop artwork, a typography poster and my own logo variations, which taught me how to communicate ideas visually.

I value patience, clear communication and the willingness to learn from feedback. My goal is to keep growing in IT, contribute to meaningful projects, and use what I learn to build a better future for myself and the people around me.
